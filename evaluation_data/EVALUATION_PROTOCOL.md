Experimental Evaluation Protocol

1. Objective

Evaluate face-detection behavior and image quality under different image acquisition conditions.

2. Test Conditions

- Normal lighting
- Low lighting
- Partial facial occlusion
- Different head orientations
- Different facial expressions

3. Controlled Variables

- Camera device
- Image resolution
- Capture environment
- Approximate camera-to-subject distance

4. Observations

For each test image, record:

- Whether a face was detected
- Image-quality observations
- Processing time, if available
- Environmental notes

5. Metrics

- Face detection rate
- Missed detections
- Processing time
- Image-quality observations

6. Privacy

- Use data from consenting volunteers.
- Store images securely.
- Use anonymized image identifiers.
- Do not interpret system output as evidence of criminal activity.

7. Limitations

Detection performance does not establish a person's identity, involvement in a crime, or guilt. Results from a small dataset may not generalize to other people or environments.
