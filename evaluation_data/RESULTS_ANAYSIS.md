Experimental Results and Analysis

1. Overview

This section documents the observations obtained during controlled computer-vision experiments. Results are reported separately for each image acquisition condition.

2. Experimental Conditions

3. Evaluation Measures

- Face detection rate
- Missed face detections
- Average processing time
- Qualitative image-quality observations

4. Results

Results will be populated only after the experiments are completed and measurements have been recorded.

5. Discussion

The results will be interpreted in relation to image quality, experimental conditions, dataset size and variability. Observations will not be treated as evidence of identity or criminal involvement.

6. Limitations

- The dataset is small.
- Results may not generalize to other participants or environments.
- Image acquisition conditions may introduce variability.
- Face detection is not identity verification.
- A computer-vision output cannot establish criminality.

7. Privacy and Data Handling

- Use data from consenting participants.
- Avoid storing unnecessary personal information.
- Restrict access to the collected images.
- Use anonymous labels in reports.
