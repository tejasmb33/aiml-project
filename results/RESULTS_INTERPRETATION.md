# Preliminary Image Quality Results

## 1. Overview

A preliminary image-quality experiment was conducted using images classified into normal and low-light conditions.

The analysis measured:
- Brightness
- Contrast
- Sharpness

## 2. Preliminary Results

| Condition | Brightness | Contrast | Sharpness | Image Count |
|----------|------------|----------|-----------|------------|
| Low light | 7.42 | 23.13 | 147.06 | 1 |
| Normal | 102.44 | 63.83 | 217.88 | 5 |

## 3. Observations

- The low-light sample has considerably lower average brightness than the normal samples.
- The low-light sample also has lower contrast and sharpness measurements than the normal-condition group averages.
- The current dataset is small and unevenly distributed across conditions.

## 4. Limitations

- Only six images were included in this preliminary analysis.
- Only one low-light image was analysed.
- These measurements are descriptive and cannot establish generalizable conclusions.
- Image-quality metrics alone do not establish the performance, accuracy or reliability of any identification system.

## 5. Future Work

- Collect more images under controlled conditions.
- Ensure balanced samples across experimental conditions.
- Record capture settings and experimental procedures.
- Perform a more comprehensive statistical analysis when sufficient data is available.

## Image Quality Measurement Results

The preliminary image quality measurements show differences between the normal-light and low-light image conditions.

- Brightness: The low-light sample recorded a substantially lower brightness value than the normal-condition samples.
- Contrast: The low-light sample showed lower contrast than the normal-condition samples.
- Sharpness: The measured sharpness values differed between the two conditions.

These observations describe the collected images only. Since the dataset is currently small and unevenly distributed, the results should not be interpreted as generalized performance findings.

The comparison graphs visualize the measured brightness, contrast, and sharpness values across the available image conditions.

Further data collection and repeated measurements are required for more reliable statistical analysis.
