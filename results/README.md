Experimental Results

This directory stores the outputs generated during the image-quality evaluation experiment.

Structure

- "graphs/" – Visualizations of experimental measurements.
- "tables/" – Tabular summaries of experimental measurements. 

Evaluation Metrics

- Brightness – Average pixel intensity of the image.
- Contrast – Variation in pixel intensity.
- Resolution – Image width and height.
- Image processing time – Time required to calculate the image-quality measurements.

Experimental Conditions

Results may be organized by controlled image conditions, including:

- Normal lighting
- Low lighting
- Partial occlusion
- Pose variation
- Facial expression variation

Purpose

The results are intended for descriptive analysis of image quality and processing behavior under controlled conditions. They are not intended to establish a person's identity or determine criminality.
