Current Research Scope

The current experimental work focuses on computer vision measurement, image acquisition conditions, reproducible observations, and analysis of processing measurements.

Any future evaluation involving human facial images must use appropriately consented participants, secure data handling, transparent evaluation and careful consideration of privacy, fairness and limitations.

Limitations
